# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Winget Manifest Studio is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone participating in the repository is expected to be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

Community members should:

- Communicate respectfully and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and the problem being solved.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Respect different levels of experience, including first-time contributors.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help repair any harm caused.
- Protect private, personal, security-sensitive, and authentication information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, discriminatory comments, personal attacks, or deliberate humiliation.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or knowingly misleading other participants.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, GitHub tokens, private vulnerability details, or other secrets publicly.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, releases, documentation, and other official project spaces. It also applies when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting a Conduct Concern

Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Contact the repository owner privately through the contact method published on the [ubidzz GitHub profile](https://github.com/ubidzz). Include links or screenshots when safe, the approximate date, and a factual description of what occurred.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Information will be shared only with people needed to evaluate or resolve the concern. Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.

Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or permanently ban someone when necessary to protect the community. Responses will consider the seriousness, frequency, impact, and context of the behavior.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker whenever another neutral reviewer is available.
